Decide when this estate will be looked at again.

## What can go wrong

A programme that ends when the first product ships leaves the rest of the estate with no date. One cutover is not the migration.

## A stronger practice

[ETSI TR 104 016](https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_tr/104000_104099/104016/01.01.01_60/tr_104016v010101p.pdf), October 2024, is the programme-office version: eleven steps, repeated each year, because an estate does not move in one cut. This site does not teach the eleven step names.

The [PQCC migration roadmap](https://pqcc.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/PQC-Migration-Roadmap-PQCC-2.pdf), May 2025, puts monitoring and evaluation last: track the migration, keep the documentation, and reassess as quantum capabilities change.

The [UK NCSC timelines](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/guidance/pqc-migration-timelines), modified 3 August 2026, put this on the 2031 row: refine the plan so there is a route to completion by 2035.

## What to ask

What is the date of the next review, and what would make you bring it forward?

## Evidence

A dated plan that still has open items.

## A weak answer

"The migration project is closed."

## A stronger answer

"The first pass covered the long-secrecy data. The next review is dated. Vendor answers that have not arrived are still on the sheet."

## Which pass

This page is the pass after the first one. [Changing algorithms again](./changing-algorithms-again.md) is the capability that makes a later pass possible.

## Options

No new algorithm is chosen here. A later pass reads the inventory again and uses [Choosing an option](../algorithm-protocol-options/choosing.md) for anything the first pass left open.
