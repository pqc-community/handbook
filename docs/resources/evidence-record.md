## When to use it

Use this record when someone asks what evidence supports a statement that a migration step is complete.

## Minimum fields

claim, record, source, date, owner, subsequent review

A cell you cannot fill is the word `unknown`.

## Filled example

| claim | record | source | date | owner | subsequent review |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The clinic recorded why health records are first for key establishment | priority record row for health records | NIST IR 8547, October 2026 | unknown | the clinic operations lead | 2026-12-01 |
