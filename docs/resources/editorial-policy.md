This page states how the handbook chooses sources, records jurisdictions, and is maintained.

## Source order

When pages cite external work, they follow this order:

1. Standards and legal requirements
2. National cyber guidance
3. Sector guidance
4. Illustrative practice

## Jurisdiction handling

**Handbook practice:** Record the timeline for the place the organisation operates or sells into, and do not copy another jurisdiction's milestone as a universal deadline.

## Owner

The maintainers of the [pqc-community/handbook](https://github.com/pqc-community/handbook) repository own the pages in this edition.

## Review interval

**Handbook practice:** Review sources every quarter and when a cited work is republished.

## Edition

November 2026.

## Changelog

- 10 November 2026 — The chooser, the routes, the learn sequence, the work products, and this voice replaced the first public edition.

Report a stale reference through the [repository issue tracker](https://github.com/pqc-community/handbook/issues).

This build does not complete two validations: a formal screen-reader audit, and observed sessions with a small organisation, a bespoke product owner, procurement, and a risk lead.
