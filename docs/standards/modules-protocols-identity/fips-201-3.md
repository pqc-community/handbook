## What it covers

FIPS 201-3 specifies architecture, identity proofing, issuance, and interoperability requirements for Personal Identity Verification (PIV) credentials used across US federal physical and logical access control. PIV credentials rely on PKI certificates, smart cards, card readers, middleware, and supporting certification authorities.

## Relationship with other standards

[NIST IR 8547](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.IR.8547), October 2026, cites FIPS 201-3 as an example of critical trust infrastructure that requires sustained coordination across hardware tokens, readers, middleware, issuance systems, and PKI because deployed credentials follow multi-year replacement cycles. [OMB M-26-15](/standards/us-federal-directives/omb-m-26-15/) likewise directs federal identity, credential, and access management modernisation toward post-quantum cryptography.
