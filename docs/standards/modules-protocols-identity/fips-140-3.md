## What it covers

FIPS 140-3 defines security requirements across four qualitative levels for hardware, software, and firmware cryptographic modules validated under the joint US-Canadian Cryptographic Module Validation Program (CMVP). It covers cryptographic boundaries, approved security functions, physical security, key management, and self-tests.

## Relationship with other standards

[NIST IR 8547](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.IR.8547), October 2026, explains that during the transition period both older quantum-vulnerable algorithms and post-quantum standards ([FIPS 203](/standards/post-quantum/fips-203/), [FIPS 204](/standards/post-quantum/fips-204/), and [FIPS 205](/standards/post-quantum/fips-205/)) coexist in validated modules, that approved hybrid key-establishment combinations remain eligible for FIPS 140 validation when at least one input comes from an approved scheme, and that NIST is revising CMVP processes under [EO 14412](/standards/us-federal-directives/eo-14412/) to accelerate validation of modules implementing post-quantum algorithms.
