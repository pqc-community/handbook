## What it covers

NIST SP 800-52 Rev. 2 provides guidance on selecting, configuring, and operating Transport Layer Security (TLS) servers and clients, including protocol version requirements (TLS 1.2 and TLS 1.3), cipher suites providing ephemeral forward secrecy, certificate profiles, and cryptographic extensions.

## Role in the post-quantum transition

**Authority recommendation:** [NIST IR 8547](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.IR.8547), October 2026, identifies TLS as an early priority for post-quantum migration because data in transit is a primary target for harvest-now, decrypt-later collection. IR 8547 notes that TLS 1.3 offers a practical near-term path to quantum-resistant key establishment — including hybrid key exchange combining ML-KEM ([FIPS 203](/standards/post-quantum/fips-203/)) with classical schemes — and states that guidance on quantum-resistant key establishment in TLS will appear in the next revision of SP 800-52.
