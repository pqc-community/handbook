Open these summaries when you need a programme-scale handbook, discovery resource, or supplier questionnaire rather than an individual algorithm specification. This handbook links directly to the Dutch PQC Migration Handbook and companion guides rather than rewriting them.

### Programme and strategy guides

| Publication | Summary | Reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dutch PQC Migration Handbook | Diagnose, plan, and execute model from AIVD, CWI, and TNO distinguishing urgent adopters, regular adopters, and suppliers. | [Dutch PQC Handbook](/standards/migration-guides/dutch-pqc-handbook/) |
| UK NCSC migration guidance | Guidance on scoping migration goals, commodity IT updated by suppliers, and bespoke or critical systems. | [UK NCSC guidance](/standards/migration-guides/uk-ncsc-pqc-guidance/) |
| EU NIS Cooperation Group roadmap | Coordinated roadmap and confidentiality risk framing for EU Member State post-quantum transitions. | [EU NIS roadmap](/standards/migration-guides/eu-nis-roadmap/) |
| ASD planning publication | Planning framing for Australian organisations preparing for post-quantum cryptography. | [ASD planning](/standards/migration-guides/asd-planning/) |
| Canadian CFDIR guide | Quantum-readiness best practices for critical-infrastructure owners and operators. | [Canadian CFDIR guide](/standards/migration-guides/canadian-cfdir-guide/) |
| ETSI TR 103 619 | Three-stage migration strategy — inventory, plan, execute — for public-key protections. | [ETSI TR 103 619](/standards/migration-guides/etsi-tr-103-619/) |
| ETSI TR 104 016 | Eleven programme-office steps for managing an organisational transition. | [ETSI TR 104 016](/standards/migration-guides/etsi-tr-104-016/) |
| PQCC migration roadmap | Four-part industry consortium roadmap covering preparation, baseline, execution, and monitoring. | [PQCC roadmap](/standards/migration-guides/pqcc-roadmap/) |
