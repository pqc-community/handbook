## What it covers

ETSI TR 103 619 structures organisational migration into three stages — inventory, plan, and execute — focused on replacing quantum-vulnerable public-key protections across IT and telecommunications estates while setting quantum key distribution and quantum random-number generators outside its scope.

## Scope and handbook use

**Assumes:** European ICT and telecommunications standards readers. **Leaves out:** Symmetric-only systems and national regulatory duties. Companion programme-office sequencing appears in [ETSI TR 104 016](/standards/migration-guides/etsi-tr-104-016/).
