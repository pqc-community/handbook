## What it covers

ETSI TR 103 619 describes how an organisation can migrate quantum-vulnerable public-key protections to quantum-safe schemes in three stages: inventory compilation, preparation of the migration plan, and migration execution. It sets quantum key distribution and quantum random-number generators outside its scope.

The report was produced by ETSI's Technical Committee on Cybersecurity (TC CYBER) and published as V1.1.1 in July 2020, the only version. ETSI Technical Reports are informative documents rather than normative specifications, so the report recommends rather than requires.

## Relationship with other standards

The report predates NIST's 2024 post-quantum standards, so it frames migration in general terms rather than naming the algorithms now standardised in [FIPS 203](/standards/post-quantum/fips-203/) for key establishment and [FIPS 204](/standards/post-quantum/fips-204/) and [FIPS 205](/standards/post-quantum/fips-205/) for signatures. It does not address symmetric-only systems or national regulatory duties.

The later [ETSI TR 104 016](/standards/migration-guides/etsi-tr-104-016/) (October 2024) describes migration as a repeatable cycle. For the inventory stage, see the handbook's [Inventory the cryptography](/migration-practices/inventory/) practice.
