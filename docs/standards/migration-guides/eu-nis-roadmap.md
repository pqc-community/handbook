## What it covers

The EU NIS Cooperation Group roadmap coordinates national PQC transition roadmaps across EU Member States, sets out pilots and phased transition expectations across high-, medium-, and low-risk use cases, and frames a long-lived confidentiality test for data protected by public-key cryptography.

## Scope and handbook use

**Assumes:** National coordinators and regulated sectors aligning with EU Member State plans. **Leaves out:** Asset-level inventory procedures inside a single enterprise. For how national roadmaps interact with asset prioritisation, see [Timelines](/learn/timelines/) and [Priority](/migration-practices/priority/).
