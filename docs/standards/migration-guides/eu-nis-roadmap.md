## What it covers

The coordinated implementation roadmap sets out how EU Member States should plan their transition to post-quantum cryptography: first steps and initial national roadmaps, then pilots and transitions banded by high-, medium- and low-risk use cases, with quantum-safe software and firmware upgrades becoming the default.

It is Part 1, Version 1.1, of the work of the PQC workstream of the NIS Cooperation Group, dated 11 June 2025 on its cover, and the first deliverable of that workstream. The European Commission published it on its digital-strategy site on 23 June 2025. It is addressed to Member States and makes recommendations; it does not itself place legal obligations on organisations.

## Relationship with other standards

The roadmap follows [Commission Recommendation (EU) 2024/1101](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reco/2024/1101/oj) of 11 April 2024, which called for a coordinated implementation roadmap for the transition. For high-risk use cases, the roadmap says quantum-vulnerable public-key cryptography "shall not be used stand-alone after the end of 2030"; for medium-risk use cases the equivalent point is the end of 2035. These are recommendations to Member States, not dates that apply universally; see [Timelines](/learn/explainers/timelines/).

The roadmap does not specify algorithms. For the positions of individual national authorities, such as Germany's BSI and France's ANSSI, on algorithms and hybrid use, see [National algorithm positions](/standards/national-algorithm-positions/). For asset-level work inside one organisation, pair it with the handbook's [Priority](/migration-practices/priority/) practice and the [Dutch PQC Migration Handbook](/standards/migration-guides/dutch-pqc-handbook/).
