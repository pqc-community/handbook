## What it covers

ASD's planning publication introduces practical preparation steps for Australian organisations — identifying where public-key cryptography protects long-lived sensitive information, engaging technology vendors, avoiding untested or proprietary cryptography, and building cryptographic agility into procurement and system refreshes.

## Scope and handbook use

**Assumes:** Organisations within ASD's stated Australian audience. **Leaves out:** Detailed supplier questionnaire lists, which are covered in the companion [vendor questions guide](/standards/migration-guides/vendor-questions/). For how ASD's planning milestone is scoped to its audience rather than serving as a universal deadline, see [Timelines](/learn/timelines/).
