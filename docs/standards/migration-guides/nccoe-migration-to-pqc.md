## What it covers

The Migration to Post-Quantum Cryptography project at NIST's National Cybersecurity Center of Excellence (NCCoE) addresses practical aspects of migration: preparing an enterprise, discovering where quantum-vulnerable cryptography is used, and testing the interoperability and performance of post-quantum implementations. Its FAQ organises common practitioner questions across six phases of a migration journey.

The project published three preliminary drafts of NIST Special Publication (SP) 1800-38: Volume A on 24 April 2023, and Volume B (Cryptographic Discovery) and Volume C (Testing Draft Standards for Interoperability and Performance) on 19 December 2023. The FAQ, last updated 18 November 2026, says there are no plans to issue second drafts or final versions; the project now shares its progress through NIST Cybersecurity White Papers (CSWP) 39 and 48. The drafts and FAQ are informative material and impose no requirements.

## Relationship with other standards

Because no final versions are planned, treat the SP 1800-38 drafts as background on discovery and laboratory testing rather than as NIST guidance in force. The project's current outputs are [NIST CSWP 39](/standards/transition-and-key-management/nist-cswp-39/) on crypto agility and CSWP 48. The project is cited in [NIST IR 8547](/standards/transition-and-key-management/nist-ir-8547/), NIST's report on the transition to post-quantum standards.

The project material is about process and testing rather than algorithm choice; the algorithms themselves are specified in [FIPS 203](/standards/post-quantum/fips-203/) for key establishment and [FIPS 204](/standards/post-quantum/fips-204/) and [FIPS 205](/standards/post-quantum/fips-205/) for signatures. For discovery work, pair the project material with the handbook's [Inventory the cryptography](/programme/inventory/) practice and the [register](/templates/records/register/).
