## What it covers

ETSI TR 104 016 sets out eleven repeatable annual steps for a programme office managing governance, stakeholder alignment, vendor tracking, pilot testing, and multi-year cryptographic transition review.

:::info[Official texts]

- **Authority:** European Telecommunications Standards Institute (ETSI)
- **Publication:** ETSI TR 104 016 V1.1.1 (October 2024)
- **Status:** Current technical report
- **Official text:** [ETSI TR 104 016 (PDF)](https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_tr/104000_104099/104016/01.01.01_60/tr_104016v010101p.pdf)

:::
