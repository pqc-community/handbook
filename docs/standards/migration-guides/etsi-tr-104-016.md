## What it covers

ETSI TR 104 016 sets out eleven repeatable annual steps for a programme office managing governance, stakeholder alignment, vendor tracking, pilot testing, and multi-year cryptographic transition review.

## Scope and handbook use

**Assumes:** Organisations operating a standing programme office across multiple business units. **Leaves out:** First-day tactical discovery steps for a small estate. Pair with [Organisation-wide route](/routes/organisation-wide/) and [Subsequent review](/migration-practices/subsequent-review/).
