## What it covers

The Canadian National Quantum-Readiness Best Practices and Guidelines set out governance, inventory, risk-assessment and supply-chain steps that organizations, particularly owners and operators of critical and digital infrastructure, can take to prepare for post-quantum cryptography. They arrange the work in stages: Stage I covers phases 0 to 2 in 2024, and Stage II covers implementation from 2025.

The document is produced by the Quantum-Readiness Working Group (QRWG) of the Canadian Forum for Digital Infrastructure Resilience (CFDIR), and the current edition is Version 04, dated July 10, 2024. It presents recommended best practices rather than imposing obligations on any audience.

## Relationship with other standards

The guide is a national best-practices document rather than a government program plan. For Government of Canada departments, the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security has published the [ITSM.40.001 roadmap](/standards/migration-guides/canada-itsm-40-001/), with its own milestones. The CFDIR guide focuses on organizational readiness rather than algorithm choice; for which algorithms national authorities recommend, see [National algorithm positions](/standards/national-algorithm-positions/), and for the underlying standards see [Post-quantum standards](/standards/post-quantum/).

Pair it with the handbook's [Which plan you need](/plan/) and [Program](/plan/program/) pages.
