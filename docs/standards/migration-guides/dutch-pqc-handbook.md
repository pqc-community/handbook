## What it covers

The PQC Migration Handbook (Guidelines for migrating to post-quantum cryptography) organizes transition work into three stages: diagnose, plan and execute. It distinguishes urgent adopters, regular adopters and cryptography experts (organizations that supply cryptographic standards or infrastructure), so that organizations can match program effort to their risk profile. A [companion Dutch government resource list](https://www.nldigitalgovernment.nl/overview/quantum-safe-cryptography/useful-resources/) points to national and international quantum-safe resources.

The Netherlands General Intelligence and Security Service (AIVD) published the extended second edition with the research institutes CWI and TNO; its document page is dated November 29, 2024, and the accompanying news release December 3, 2024. It is guidance for organizations planning a migration, not a legal requirement.

## Relationship with other standards

The handbook is a program methodology rather than an algorithm specification; for the standards it points migration teams toward, see [Post-quantum standards](/standards/post-quantum/), and for national positions on algorithms and hybrids, see [National algorithm positions](/standards/national-algorithm-positions/). It does not set legal obligations; EU Member State planning milestones come from the [EU NIS Cooperation Group roadmap](/standards/migration-guides/eu-nis-roadmap/).

Rather than rewriting its methodology, this handbook links to it alongside its own [Which plan you need](/plan/) page and [Inventory the cryptography](/plan/program/inventory/) practice.
