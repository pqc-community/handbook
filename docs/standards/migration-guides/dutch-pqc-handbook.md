## What it covers

The Dutch PQC Migration Handbook organises transition work into three stages — diagnose, plan, and execute — and distinguishes urgent adopters, regular adopters, and cryptography suppliers so organisations match programme effort to their risk profile. A companion Dutch government web page curates pointers to national and international quantum-safe resources.

## Scope and handbook use

**Assumes:** A national-strategy audience in the Netherlands or an organisation adopting the same three-stage programme model elsewhere. **Leaves out:** Jurisdiction-specific legal obligations outside the Netherlands. Rather than rewriting its programme methodology, this handbook points to it directly alongside [Diagnose](/migration-practices/diagnose/).
