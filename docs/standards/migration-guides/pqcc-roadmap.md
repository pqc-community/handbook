## What it covers

The Post-Quantum Cryptography Coalition's migration roadmap is a short planning guide that organises an organisation's move to post-quantum cryptography into four categories, each with activities and desired outcomes: preparation (setting migration aims, assigning a migration lead, identifying stakeholders and aligning them through strategic messaging), baseline understanding (inventory, prioritised assets, and required resources and budget), planning and execution (working with system vendors and internal system owners to acquire or develop post-quantum solutions and implement them), and monitoring and evaluation (tracking progress and reassessing cryptographic security as quantum capabilities evolve). The roadmap says the categories can run concurrently or in a staggered order, and that how an organisation applies them depends on factors such as the shelf-life and volume of its critical data and its budget.

The coalition is a non-governmental community of technologists, researchers and practitioners; the document is published under MITRE's copyright and approved for public release. MITRE's release announcing it addresses chief information officers and chief information security officers in organisations of all sizes. It is voluntary guidance with no regulatory standing and binds no audience.

## Relationship with other standards

The roadmap sorts organisations into urgent adopters, which handle highly sensitive data or assets, and regular adopters — the same distinction the [Dutch PQC Migration Handbook](/standards/migration-guides/dutch-pqc-handbook/) uses. It cites the NIST post-quantum standards ([FIPS 203](/standards/post-quantum/fips-203/), [FIPS 204](/standards/post-quantum/fips-204/) and [FIPS 205](/standards/post-quantum/fips-205/)), and MITRE's release says it builds on those standards and on the [NCCoE Migration to PQC](/standards/migration-guides/nccoe-migration-to-pqc/) project. Because it was published in May 2025, it refers to the initial public draft of NIST IR 8547; use the final [NIST IR 8547](/standards/transition-and-key-management/nist-ir-8547/) (October 2026) for NIST's current transition schedule.

The roadmap does not set jurisdiction-specific legal duties or regulator milestones; for those, see the national guides in [Post-quantum migration guides](/standards/migration-guides/) and [Why migration timelines differ](/learn/explainers/why-timelines-differ/). For a programme method from national authorities, compare [ASD's planning guidance](/standards/migration-guides/asd-planning/) and the [UK NCSC guidance](/standards/migration-guides/ncsc-migration-timelines/).
