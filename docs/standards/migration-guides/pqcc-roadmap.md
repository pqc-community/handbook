## What it covers

The PQCC migration roadmap groups enterprise transition work into four categories: preparation, baseline understanding, planning and execution, and ongoing monitoring and evaluation.

## Scope and handbook use

**Assumes:** Multi-sector enterprise programme teams looking for a vendor-neutral checklist. **Leaves out:** Jurisdiction-specific legal duties and regulator milestones.
