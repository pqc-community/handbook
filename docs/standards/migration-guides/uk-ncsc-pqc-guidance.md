## What it covers

The UK NCSC guidance frames how large organisations, operators of critical national infrastructure, and organisations maintaining bespoke IT scope discovery, supplier engagement, long-lived hardware roots of trust, and staged migration. Crucially, it separates commodity IT — standard browsers, operating systems, and mobile devices that follow normal supplier updates — from custom or specialised systems that require active planning.

## Scope and handbook use

**Assumes:** Organisations operating in or selling into the United Kingdom. **Leaves out:** Detailed algorithm parameter tables and enterprise questionnaire templates. For how NCSC distinguishes audiences and when its milestones apply, see [Timelines](/learn/timelines/) and [Commodity software](/migration-practices/commodity-software/).
