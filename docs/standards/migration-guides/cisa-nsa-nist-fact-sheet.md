## What it covers

The fact sheet Quantum-Readiness: Migration to Post-Quantum Cryptography makes a brief case for starting a quantum-readiness roadmap now: assembling an inventory of quantum-vulnerable cryptography, assessing which data has long confidentiality requirements, and asking technology vendors how they plan to deliver NIST-standardised post-quantum cryptography.

It was issued jointly by the US Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA), National Security Agency (NSA) and National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), and its footer dates it as of 17 August 2023. It is addressed to organisations, especially those supporting critical infrastructure. It urges action but imposes no requirements.

## Relationship with other standards

The fact sheet predates the August 2024 publication of [FIPS 203](/standards/post-quantum/fips-203/) for key establishment and [FIPS 204](/standards/post-quantum/fips-204/) and [FIPS 205](/standards/post-quantum/fips-205/) for signatures, so it refers to standards still in preparation. For NIST's current transition position, see [NIST IR 8547](/standards/transition-and-key-management/nist-ir-8547/).

It is an opening document for executives and programme leads; it does not give discovery field lists or sector milestones. Pair it with the [NCCoE Migration to PQC project](/standards/migration-guides/nccoe-migration-to-pqc/), the handbook's [Start here](/start-here/) page and its [Work with suppliers](/programme/suppliers/) practice.
