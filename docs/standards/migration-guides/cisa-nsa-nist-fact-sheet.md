## What it covers

The joint CISA, NSA, and NIST fact sheet makes a brief official case for building a quantum-readiness roadmap now: assembling an inventory of quantum-vulnerable cryptography, assessing which data has long confidentiality requirements, and asking technology vendors how they plan to deliver NIST-standardised post-quantum cryptography.

## Scope and handbook use

**Assumes:** Executives and programme leads opening a first planning conversation, particularly in critical infrastructure or US-aligned enterprises. **Leaves out:** Detailed discovery field lists and European regulatory plans. Pair with [Diagnose](/migration-practices/diagnose/) and [Ask a supplier](/routes/ask-a-supplier/).
