## What it covers

NIST SP 800-131A defines the approval states — acceptable, deprecated, legacy use, and disallowed — for cryptographic algorithms and key lengths used to protect sensitive unclassified information under NIST standards. It covers encryption, key establishment, digital signatures, hash functions, MACs, and random number generation.

## Relationship with other standards

[NIST IR 8547](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.IR.8547), October 2026, notes that NIST uses SP 800-131A to carry formal algorithm approval transitions, including retiring remaining 112-bit symmetric algorithms alongside the move away from quantum-vulnerable public-key schemes. IR 8547 specifically flags the Revision 3 initial public draft and notes that future updates will align approval statuses with the post-quantum transition.
