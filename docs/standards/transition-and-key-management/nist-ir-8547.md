## What it covers

NIST IR 8547 explains the vulnerability of classical public-key cryptography to a cryptanalytically relevant quantum computer, surveys the standards and technology layers affected, and sets out NIST's plan for deprecating and disallowing quantum-vulnerable public-key algorithms in NIST standards and guidelines. It also summarises hybrid constructions, use-case trade-offs across code signing, authentication, network protocols, and messaging, and the relative resilience of existing symmetric primitives.
