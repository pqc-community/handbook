## What it covers

NIST SP 800-57 Part 1 sets out general key-management principles across generation, distribution, storage, use, backup, archival, and destruction of cryptographic keys. It defines key types, cryptoperiod considerations, protection of keying material, and classical security-strength comparisons across symmetric and public-key algorithms.

## Role in the post-quantum transition

**Authority recommendation:** [NIST IR 8547](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.IR.8547), October 2026, cites SP 800-57 Part 1 for NIST's earlier classical key-strength transition projections and explains how the post-quantum transition supersedes simple classical key-size increases so organisations migrate once to quantum-resistant schemes rather than staging an intermediate classical upgrade.
