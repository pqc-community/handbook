## What it covers

NIST SP 800-56A specifies pair-wise key-agreement schemes based on discrete logarithms over finite fields and elliptic curves, including Diffie-Hellman (DH), Elliptic Curve Diffie-Hellman (ECDH), and Menezes-Qu-Vanstone (MQV) constructions. Both parties contribute information so neither participant independently predetermines the shared secret.

## Relationship with other standards

[NIST IR 8547](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.IR.8547), October 2026, identifies finite-field and elliptic-curve DH and MQV in SP 800-56A as quantum-vulnerable and notes that harvest-now, decrypt-later risk makes key establishment an early migration priority. SP 800-56A also plays a transitional role in hybrid key establishment, where an ECDH shared secret from SP 800-56A is combined with an ML-KEM shared secret key from [FIPS 203](/standards/post-quantum/fips-203/).
