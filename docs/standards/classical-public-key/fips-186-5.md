## What it covers

FIPS 186-5 specifies NIST's classical digital signature algorithms: the Elliptic Curve Digital Signature Algorithm (ECDSA), the Edwards-Curve Digital Signature Algorithm (EdDSA, referencing RFC 8032), and RSA signature schemes (referencing RFC 8017 PKCS #1 v2.2). Elliptic curve domain parameters used with FIPS 186-5 are defined separately in [NIST SP 800-186](/standards/classical-public-key/sp-800-186/).

## Role in the post-quantum transition

**Authority recommendation:** [NIST IR 8547](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.IR.8547), October 2026, classifies all signature families in FIPS 186-5 — ECDSA, EdDSA, and RSA — as quantum-vulnerable and places them on NIST's transition path toward post-quantum replacements in [FIPS 204](/standards/post-quantum/fips-204/), [FIPS 205](/standards/post-quantum/fips-205/), and [NIST SP 800-208](/standards/post-quantum/sp-800-208/). Finding FIPS 186-5 signatures in certificates, firmware, and code-signing pipelines is a core task during [Inventory](/migration-practices/inventory/).
