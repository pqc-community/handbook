## What it covers

NIST SP 800-56B specifies pair-wise key-establishment schemes relying on RSA integer factorisation. Unlike [NIST SP 800-56A](/standards/classical-public-key/sp-800-56a/), it includes both RSA key-transport schemes — where the sender chooses secret keying material and encrypts it under the receiver's public key — and RSA-based key-agreement schemes.

:::info[Official texts]

- **Authority:** National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)
- **Publication:** NIST Special Publication 800-56B Rev. 2 (2019)
- **Status:** Final Special Publication (classical, quantum-vulnerable)
- **Official text:** [NIST publication page via DOI (HTML)](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.SP.800-56Br2)

:::

## Relationship with other standards

[NIST IR 8547](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.IR.8547), October 2026, classifies RSA key-establishment schemes in SP 800-56B as quantum-vulnerable and schedules their retirement alongside [NIST SP 800-56A](/standards/classical-public-key/sp-800-56a/) in favour of post-quantum key encapsulation in [FIPS 203](/standards/post-quantum/fips-203/). Static RSA key transport also lacks forward secrecy if the long-term private key is retained.
