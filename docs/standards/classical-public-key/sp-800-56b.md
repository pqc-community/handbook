## What it covers

NIST Special Publication (SP) 800-56B specifies pair-wise key-establishment schemes based on RSA and the difficulty of integer factorisation. Unlike [NIST SP 800-56A](/standards/classical-public-key/sp-800-56a/), it includes RSA key-transport schemes, in which the sender chooses the secret keying material and encrypts it under the receiver's public key, as well as RSA-based key-agreement schemes. SP 800-56B covers key establishment only; RSA signatures are covered by [FIPS 186-5](/standards/classical-public-key/fips-186-5/).

NIST issues SPs as guidance and recommendations for US federal agencies; others widely use them as a reference. The current version is Revision 2, which NIST reaffirmed as current in January 2026.

## Relationship with other standards

[NIST IR 8547](/standards/transition-and-key-management/nist-ir-8547/), final October 2026, lists RSA key establishment in SP 800-56B as quantum-vulnerable, on the same schedule as SP 800-56A, and names [FIPS 203](/standards/post-quantum/fips-203/) (ML-KEM) as the only approved post-quantum key-establishment scheme at the time of the report. For US federal high value assets and high-impact systems, [EO 14412](/standards/us-government-requirements/eo-14412/) sets 31 December 2030 for quantum-resistant key establishment, and [OMB M-26-15](/standards/us-government-requirements/omb-m-26-15/) phases agencies' work to meet it; other authorities set their own dates (see [Why migration timelines differ](/learn/explainers/why-timelines-differ/)). For how key-establishment design relates to recorded traffic, see [Forward secrecy and recorded traffic](/learn/explainers/forward-secrecy/).

In hybrid key establishment, IR 8547 describes the [SP 800-56C](/standards/symmetric-and-hash/sp-800-56c/) generic composite, in which a shared secret Z from SP 800-56A or SP 800-56B is concatenated with a second secret T before key derivation; T may come from a disallowed algorithm (§3.2.1). RSA signatures are a separate migration with different drivers; see [FIPS 186-5](/standards/classical-public-key/fips-186-5/).
