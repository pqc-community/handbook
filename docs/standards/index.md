Use these reference summaries to find the official publication page or PDF for each cryptographic standard, policy directive, and migration guide cited in this handbook, together with what it covers and how it fits into a post-quantum transition.

Publications are grouped by their role in the transition:

- **[Post-quantum cryptography](/standards/post-quantum/)**: NIST post-quantum key-encapsulation and digital signature standards, stateful hash-based signatures, KEM combiner recommendations, and standardisation status reports.
- **[Transition and key management](/standards/transition-and-key-management/)**: NIST transition guidance, algorithm and key-length transition rules, and general cryptographic key-management recommendations.
- **[Classical public-key (transitioning)](/standards/classical-public-key/)**: Quantum-vulnerable signature, curve, and pair-wise key-establishment standards that post-quantum schemes replace.
- **[Symmetric, hash, and KDF primitives](/standards/symmetric-and-primitives/)**: Block ciphers, lightweight cryptography, hash functions, extendable-output functions, MACs, key-derivation methods, and random bit generators.
- **[Modules, protocols, and identity](/standards/modules-protocols-identity/)**: Cryptographic module validation, TLS configuration, and federal personal identity verification standards.
- **[US federal directives](/standards/us-federal-directives/)**: White House and Office of Management and Budget directives cited in NIST transition guidance.
- **[Migration guides](/standards/migration-guides/)**: National, ETSI, and consortium guides covering programme setup, cryptographic discovery, and supplier engagement.

For how migration dates differ from published algorithm standards, see [Timelines](/learn/timelines/).
