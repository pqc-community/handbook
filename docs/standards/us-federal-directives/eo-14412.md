## What it covers

Executive Order 14412 establishes the US federal policy framework to accelerate the protection of government systems against quantum cryptographic attacks, pair transition guidance with agency execution rules, and streamline validation of post-quantum cryptographic modules.

## Relationship with other standards

[NIST IR 8547 (PDF)](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.IR.8547), October 2026, notes that IR 8547 was issued in alignment with EO 14412, that EO 14412 directs NIST to revise Cryptographic Module Validation Program ([FIPS 140-3](/standards/modules-protocols-identity/fips-140-3/)) processes to accelerate validation of modules implementing post-quantum algorithms, and that its agency migration expectations are operationalised by [OMB M-26-15](/standards/us-federal-directives/omb-m-26-15/).
