## What it covers

Executive Order 14306 amends Executive Order 13694 and Executive Order 14144 to sustain selected federal cybersecurity initiatives across protocol modernisation, cryptographic readiness, and agency cyber defence.

:::info[Official texts]

- [EO 14306: Sustaining Select Efforts to Strengthen the Nation's Cybersecurity](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/DCPD-202500669)

:::

## Relationship with other standards

[NIST IR 8547](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.IR.8547), October 2026, cites EO 14306 alongside [OMB M-26-15](/standards/us-federal-directives/omb-m-26-15/) for the US federal agency requirement to support TLS 1.3 as a prerequisite for deploying quantum-resistant key establishment across federal network traffic.
