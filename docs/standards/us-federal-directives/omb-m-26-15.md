## What it covers

OMB Memorandum M-26-15 operationalises [EO 14412](/standards/us-federal-directives/eo-14412/) for US federal agencies. It directs a risk-prioritised migration focusing early attention on federal high-value assets, high-impact systems, systems protecting data expected to remain mission-sensitive, TLS 1.3 readiness, federal identity and credential management (FICAM), and replacement or decommissioning of legacy systems that cannot support post-quantum cryptography.

## Relationship with other standards

[NIST IR 8547 (PDF)](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.IR.8547), October 2026, explains that while NIST's general algorithm standards define broad deprecation and disallowance horizons, US federal agencies covered by OMB M-26-15 are subject to earlier risk-prioritised requirements for priority federal systems. For how audience-specific government directives differ from general standards, see [Timelines](/learn/timelines/).
