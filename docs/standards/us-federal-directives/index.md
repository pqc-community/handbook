These US presidential and Office of Management and Budget directives establish policy direction for US federal civilian agencies and inform the federal transition context cited in [NIST IR 8547](/standards/transition-and-key-management/nist-ir-8547/). Each directive applies to its stated US federal audience rather than every private organisation worldwide; see [Timelines](/learn/timelines/).

| Publication | Summary | Reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NSM-10 | 2022 National Security Memorandum initiating coordinated US federal planning to mitigate quantum risks to vulnerable cryptography. | [NSM-10](/standards/us-federal-directives/nsm-10/) |
| EO 14306 | June 2025 Executive Order sustaining select federal cybersecurity efforts, including federal TLS 1.3 adoption. | [EO 14306](/standards/us-federal-directives/eo-14306/) |
| EO 14412 | June 2026 Executive Order establishing policy to accelerate protection against advanced cryptographic attacks and speed CMVP validation. | [EO 14412](/standards/us-federal-directives/eo-14412/) |
| OMB M-26-15 | June 2026 OMB memorandum operationalising federal risk-prioritised execution of the post-quantum migration. | [OMB M-26-15](/standards/us-federal-directives/omb-m-26-15/) |
