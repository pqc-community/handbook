## What it covers

NIST SP 800-227 provides implementation and operational guidance for key-encapsulation mechanisms, such as ML-KEM in [FIPS 203](/standards/post-quantum/fips-203/). It covers security properties of KEMs, safe handling of keys and ciphertexts, and key combiners that merge two or more shared secret keys into a single combined key.

## Role in the post-quantum transition

[NIST IR 8547](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.IR.8547), October 2026, points to SP 800-227 for guidance on key combiners used in post-quantum and classical hybrid key-establishment protocols. Where at least one input to an approved composite technique comes from an approved key-establishment scheme, IR 8547 notes that the combination can be FIPS 140-validated.
