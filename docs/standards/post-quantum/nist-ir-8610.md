## What it covers

NIST Internal Report (IR) 8610 describes the evaluation criteria and selection process for the second round of NIST's call for additional post-quantum digital signature schemes. NIST says any scheme eventually selected would augment its existing signature standards — FIPS 204, FIPS 205, FIPS 186-5 and SP 800-208 — rather than replace them. Based on public feedback and internal reviews, NIST advanced nine candidates to a third round: FAEST, HAWK, MAYO, MQOM, QR-UOV, SDitH, SNOVA, SQIsign and UOV.

IR 8610 is an informative report from the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST); it does not itself impose requirements, and none of the third-round candidates is an approved standard.

## Relationship with other standards

IR 8610 succeeds [NIST IR 8528](/standards/post-quantum/nist-ir-8528/) as the current status report for the additional-signatures track. This track is separate from the FALCON-derived signature FIPS that [NIST IR 8547](/standards/transition-and-key-management/nist-ir-8547/) says is under development.

Approved post-quantum signatures today are [FIPS 204](/standards/post-quantum/fips-204/), [FIPS 205](/standards/post-quantum/fips-205/) and [SP 800-208](/standards/post-quantum/sp-800-208/). The third-round candidates are relevant to horizon scanning and to designing systems that can [change algorithms again](/migration-practices/changing-algorithms-again/), rather than to procurement requirements.
