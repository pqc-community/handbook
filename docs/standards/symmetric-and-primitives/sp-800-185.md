## What it covers

NIST SP 800-185 defines four cryptographic functions derived from the Keccak permutation in [FIPS 202](/standards/symmetric-and-primitives/fips-202/): cSHAKE (a customisable extendable-output function supporting domain separation), KMAC (Keyed Message Authentication Code with variable-length output), TupleHash (unambiguous hashing of sequences of strings), and ParallelHash.

## Role in the post-quantum transition

**Authority recommendation:** [NIST IR 8547](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.IR.8547), October 2026, groups SP 800-185 with NIST's continuing XOF and symmetric standards that remain resilient against known quantum attacks at 128-bit and 256-bit security levels, and KMAC is also referenced in NIST key-derivation and KEM combiner designs such as [NIST SP 800-108](/standards/symmetric-and-primitives/sp-800-108/) and [NIST SP 800-227](/standards/post-quantum/sp-800-227/).
