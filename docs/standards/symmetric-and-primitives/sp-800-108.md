## What it covers

NIST SP 800-108 specifies key-derivation functions (in counter, feedback, and double-pipeline iteration modes) that use a pseudorandom function — such as HMAC, AES-CMAC, or KMAC — to derive one or more symmetric keys from an already established cryptographic key, such as a shared secret key output by [FIPS 203](/standards/post-quantum/fips-203/).

## Role in the post-quantum transition

**Authority recommendation:** [NIST IR 8547](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.IR.8547), October 2026, lists SP 800-108 among NIST's symmetric KDF standards that remain resilient to known quantum attacks when used with keys of at least 128 bits of security and do not require transition away as part of post-quantum migration.
