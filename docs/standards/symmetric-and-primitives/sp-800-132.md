## What it covers

NIST SP 800-132 specifies password-based key derivation (PBKDF2) using an approved pseudorandom function such as HMAC together with a salt and iteration count to derive symmetric keys for encrypting or authenticating stored data.

## Role in the post-quantum transition

[NIST IR 8547](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.IR.8547), October 2026, groups SP 800-132 with NIST's symmetric KDF publications that are not vulnerable to Shor's algorithm and are not scheduled for replacement as part of the public-key post-quantum transition, though password entropy and iteration parameters remain governed by classical brute-force resistance.
