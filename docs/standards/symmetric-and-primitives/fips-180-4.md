## What it covers

FIPS 180-4 specifies SHA-1 and the SHA-2 family of fixed-length cryptographic hash functions: SHA-224, SHA-256, SHA-384, SHA-512, SHA-512/224, and SHA-512/256. Hash digests from FIPS 180-4 are used across digital signature pre-hashing, HMAC, key derivation, and hash-based signatures.

## Role in the post-quantum transition

[NIST IR 8547](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.IR.8547), October 2026, confirms that SHA-256, SHA-384, SHA-512, and SHA-512/256 remain approved and meet NIST post-quantum security categories 2 through 5, including as underlying primitives inside [FIPS 205](/standards/post-quantum/fips-205/) and [NIST SP 800-208](/standards/post-quantum/sp-800-208/). IR 8547 notes that SHA-1 is already deprecated and scheduled for disallowance in FIPS-validated modules, and that 112-bit collision-strength variants (SHA-224 and SHA-512/224) fall below Category 1.
