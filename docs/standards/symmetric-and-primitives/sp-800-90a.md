## What it covers

NIST SP 800-90A specifies approved deterministic random bit generators (Hash_DRBG, HMAC_DRBG, and CTR_DRBG) seeded from approved entropy sources to generate random bits for cryptographic key generation, nonces, and encapsulation randomness.

## Role in the post-quantum transition

[NIST IR 8547](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.IR.8547), October 2026, includes SP 800-90A among symmetric primitives that remain secure against known quantum attacks without replacement. IR 8547 also notes an implementation detail for [FIPS 204](/standards/post-quantum/fips-204/): the security category of ML-DSA-44 drops from Category 2 to Category 1 if paired with an approved random bit generator providing less than 192 bits of security strength.
