## What it covers

NIST SP 800-232 specifies lightweight symmetric primitives from the Ascon family for resource-constrained hardware and embedded devices: Ascon-AEAD128 for authenticated encryption with associated data, Ascon-Hash256 for fixed-length hashing, and Ascon-XOF128 and Ascon-CXOF128 for extendable-output functions.

## Role in the post-quantum transition

**Authority recommendation:** [NIST IR 8547](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.IR.8547), October 2026, lists the SP 800-232 Ascon variants in its tables of approved symmetric AEAD, hash, and XOF primitives that meet at least NIST post-quantum security category 1 (or category 2 for hashing and adequately sized XOF outputs) and do not require replacement in the post-quantum migration.
