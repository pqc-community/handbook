## What it covers

FIPS 198-1 specifies HMAC, a mechanism for message authentication that combines a shared secret key with an approved cryptographic hash function from [FIPS 180-4](/standards/symmetric-and-primitives/fips-180-4/) or [FIPS 202](/standards/symmetric-and-primitives/fips-202/). HMAC verifies data integrity and shared-key authenticity and serves as a building block inside key-derivation functions and DRBGs.

## Role in the post-quantum transition

[NIST IR 8547](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.IR.8547), October 2026, includes symmetric message authentication among primitives that are significantly less vulnerable to quantum attack than public-key cryptography and do not need to be replaced when paired with an approved hash function and adequate key length. Unlike a digital signature, HMAC requires both parties to share the secret key.
