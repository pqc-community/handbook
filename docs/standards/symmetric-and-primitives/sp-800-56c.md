## What it covers

NIST SP 800-56C specifies one-step and two-step (extract-then-expand) key-derivation methods using hash functions, HMAC, or KMAC to derive secret keying material from a shared secret produced during key establishment. Crucially, it permits auxiliary shared secrets to be concatenated into the derivation input alongside a NIST-approved shared secret.

## Relationship with other standards

[NIST IR 8547](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.IR.8547), October 2026, highlights SP 800-56C both as a continuing quantum-resilient symmetric KDF standard and as NIST's existing approved mechanism for generic composite (hybrid) key establishment — combining a classical shared secret from [NIST SP 800-56A](/standards/classical-public-key/sp-800-56a/) or [NIST SP 800-56B](/standards/classical-public-key/sp-800-56b/) with a post-quantum shared secret such as ML-KEM from [FIPS 203](/standards/post-quantum/fips-203/).
