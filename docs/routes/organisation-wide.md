This route is for a migration lead who is coordinating many assets across the organisation. Use this route to coordinate the programme, and follow the asset route for each system as well. Coordinating many assets is not a reason to leave the asset route.

Each role supplies one input to the plan:

| Role | Input |
| --- | --- |
| Executive sponsor | The decision to proceed, in an accepted [decision note](/resources/records/decision-note/), and the agreed [programme charter](/resources/records/programme-charter/) |
| Migration lead | The migration plan |
| Asset owners | The system and its unknowns |
| Procurement | The supplier questions |
| Legal or risk | The confidentiality period and the applicable timeline |
| Operations | The change window and the rollback owner |
| Suppliers | The product, version, and the answer |

Work through the eight steps in this order:

1. Decide whether to act, and name a sponsor and migration lead.
2. Set up the programme: roles, scope, first budget line, and reporting.
3. Find cryptographic dependencies and record unknowns.
4. Prioritise by job, confidentiality period, verifier constraints, and lead time.
5. Plan dates against the timelines that apply to you.
6. Work with existing suppliers and buy post-quantum-ready products.
7. Make and verify each change, and keep the ability to change again.
8. Review, report to the sponsor, and reopen records on triggers.

These steps follow the same broad sequence as the national [migration guides](/standards/migration-guides/) summarised in Standards — find the cryptography, assess and prioritise it, implement, and communicate — with programme setup and dated planning made explicit because they decide who does the work and by when.

A large bespoke estate, critical national infrastructure, or a regulated system needs a specialist as well as this route.

Then read these pages in order:

1. [Migration plan](/resources/records/migration-plan/)
2. [Decide whether to act](/migration-practices/diagnose/)
3. [Set up the programme](/migration-practices/programme/)
4. [Inventory the cryptography](/migration-practices/inventory/)
5. [Decide what to migrate first](/migration-practices/priority/)
6. [Plan the migration](/migration-practices/plan/)
7. [Change record](/resources/records/change-record/)
8. [Review and report](/migration-practices/subsequent-review/)

The output is a [migration plan](/resources/records/migration-plan/) with an owner, an order, and a subsequent-review date for each asset and job.
