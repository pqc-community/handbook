Use this section when a government publication, a customer question, or a supplier claim mentions a migration date and you need to know which authority's timeline applies to your organisation.

[Dates](./dates.md) compares the milestones this edition records. [Migration guides](./migration-guides.md) lists programme-scale guides. Country and agency names stay on those pages; they are not the site navigation.

**Handbook practice:** A timeline applies when the organisation operates or sells in that jurisdiction. The next page is [Dates](/standards-timelines/dates/).

### Recording applicable migration timelines

**[UK NCSC timelines](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/guidance/pqc-migration-timelines)**, modified 3 August 2026. **Covers:** Milestones for 2028, 2031, and 2035, who the guidance is primarily for, and the separate path for commodity IT updated by suppliers. **Assumes:** A United Kingdom audience, or an organisation that sells into the United Kingdom. **Leaves out:** Algorithm choices and protocol configuration. **Status:** Current guidance. **Last checked:** 10 November 2026.

**[EU NIS Cooperation Group roadmap](https://securitydelta.nl/media/com_hsd/report/750/document/Roadmap-on-postquantum-cryptography-PzBJxNUYyeuEdVUacWL696DofZQ-117507.pdf)**, version 1.1, 11 June 2025. **Covers:** Coordinated Member State milestones through 2035 and a public-key confidentiality risk test. **Assumes:** Government planners coordinating national transition. **Leaves out:** Asset-level inventories for a single enterprise. **Status:** Current government plan. **Last checked:** 10 November 2026.

**[NIST IR 8547](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.IR.8547)**, October 2026. **Covers:** The NIST standards timeline, US federal direction in the same report, and technical transition guidance for NIST algorithm users. **Assumes:** Organisations that follow NIST standards or need the federal dates named in the report. **Leaves out:** Non-US legal obligations outside the federal rows. **Status:** Current report. **Last checked:** 10 November 2026.

**[ASD planning publication](https://www.cyber.gov.au/publication/planning-for-post-quantum-cryptography)**. **Covers:** Planning steps for Australian organisations and a planning milestone at the end of 2030 for the publication's audience. **Assumes:** Readers in ASD's audience. **Leaves out:** Detailed supplier questionnaires (the vendor guide covers those). **Status:** Current publication. **Last checked:** 10 November 2026. **Handbook practice:** End of 2030 is ASD's planning milestone for its audience, not a universal deadline.

**[Canadian CFDIR quantum-readiness guide](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/spectrum-management-telecommunications/sites/default/files/documents/Quantum-Readiness%20Best%20Practices%20-%20v04%20-%2010%20July%202024.pdf)**, 10 July 2024. **Covers:** Leadership actions critical-infrastructure operators can take before specialist cryptographers are engaged. **Assumes:** Canadian critical-infrastructure context or an organisation adapting the guide elsewhere. **Leaves out:** Named algorithm parameters and year-by-year government milestones. **Status:** Current guide. **Last checked:** 10 November 2026.
