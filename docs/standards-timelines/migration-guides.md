Use this page when you need the programme-scale guide, not another summary of it.

This book does not replace these works. Each entry states what the work covers, what it assumes, what it leaves out, whether it is current or preliminary, and when it was last checked. Follow the link for the full text.

### Recording applicable migration timelines

**Audience:** Risk owners and programme leads choosing which dated milestone to record.

**[UK NCSC timelines](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/guidance/pqc-migration-timelines)**, modified 3 August 2026. **Covers:** Milestones for 2028, 2031, and 2035, audience scope, and commodity IT updated by suppliers. **Assumes:** United Kingdom jurisdiction or sales into the United Kingdom. **Leaves out:** Enterprise inventories and vendor questionnaires. **Status:** Current guidance. **Last checked:** 10 November 2026.

**[EU NIS Cooperation Group roadmap](https://securitydelta.nl/media/com_hsd/report/750/document/Roadmap-on-postquantum-cryptography-PzBJxNUYyeuEdVUacWL696DofZQ-117507.pdf)**, version 1.1, 11 June 2025. **Covers:** Member State coordination milestones and risk bands for public-key confidentiality. **Assumes:** National coordinators. **Leaves out:** Single-organisation priority methods. **Status:** Current government plan. **Last checked:** 10 November 2026.

**[NIST IR 8547](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.IR.8547)**, October 2026. **Covers:** Standards deprecation and disallowance dates, federal asset directions, and migration concepts for NIST users. **Assumes:** Engagement with NIST standards or US federal systems named in the report. **Leaves out:** Procurement law outside the United States. **Status:** Current report. **Last checked:** 10 November 2026.

**[ASD planning publication](https://www.cyber.gov.au/publication/planning-for-post-quantum-cryptography)**. **Covers:** Planning framing for Australian organisations and a planning milestone at the end of 2030. **Assumes:** ASD's stated audience. **Leaves out:** Supplier question templates. **Status:** Current publication. **Last checked:** 10 November 2026. **Handbook practice:** End of 2030 is ASD's planning milestone for its audience, not a universal deadline.

**[Canadian CFDIR quantum-readiness guide](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/spectrum-management-telecommunications/sites/default/files/documents/Quantum-Readiness%20Best%20Practices%20-%20v04%20-%2010%20July%202024.pdf)**, 10 July 2024. **Covers:** Leadership actions for critical-infrastructure readiness. **Assumes:** Canadian critical-infrastructure owners and operators, or adapters elsewhere. **Leaves out:** Algorithm parameter tables. **Status:** Current guide. **Last checked:** 10 November 2026.

### Opening a programme

**Audience:** Executives and migration leads at the first planning meeting.

**[CISA, NSA, and NIST fact sheet](https://www.nccoe.nist.gov/sites/default/files/2023-08/quantum-readiness-fact-sheet.pdf)**, as of 17 August 2023. **Covers:** A short official case for inventory, suppliers, and supply chain. **Assumes:** United States government alignment and enterprise scale. **Leaves out:** European legal milestones. **Status:** Current fact sheet as dated. **Last checked:** 10 November 2026.

**[NCCoE migration FAQ](https://pages.nist.gov/nccoe-migration-post-quantum-cryptography/FAQ/index.html)**, last updated 5 November 2026. **Covers:** Answers grouped into six phases of a migration journey, and pointers to NCCoE practice materials. **Assumes:** Readers new to post-quantum migration terms. **Leaves out:** Finished NIST SP 1800-38 publications (see discovery group). **Status:** Current FAQ. **Last checked:** 5 November 2026.

### Cryptographic discovery and inventory

**Audience:** Asset owners and security engineers building the first inventory.

**[NIST SP 1800-38A preliminary draft](https://www.nccoe.nist.gov/sites/default/files/2023-04/pqc-migration-nist-sp-1800-38a-preliminary-draft.pdf)**, April 2023. **Covers:** Preparation before implementing quantum-safe cryptography in an enterprise. **Assumes:** An enterprise programme office. **Leaves out:** Final publication status. **Status:** Preliminary draft; the NCCoE FAQ, last updated 5 November 2026, states there are no current plans to issue second drafts or final versions. **Last checked:** 10 November 2026.

**[NIST SP 1800-38B preliminary draft](https://www.nccoe.nist.gov/sites/default/files/2023-12/pqc-migration-nist-sp-1800-38b-preliminary-draft.pdf)**, December 2023. **Covers:** Cryptographic discovery, including developing cryptography bill of materials (CBOM) field lists. **Assumes:** Technical staff running discovery. **Leaves out:** Finished standard status. **Status:** Preliminary draft; the NCCoE FAQ, last updated 5 November 2026, states there are no current plans to issue second drafts or final versions. **Last checked:** 10 November 2026.

**[NIST SP 1800-38C preliminary draft](https://www.nccoe.nist.gov/sites/default/files/2023-12/pqc-migration-nist-sp-1800-38c-preliminary-draft.pdf)**, December 2023. **Covers:** Testing draft standards in a laboratory setting. **Assumes:** Teams with test environments. **Leaves out:** Production cutover runbooks. **Status:** Preliminary draft; the NCCoE FAQ, last updated 5 November 2026, states there are no current plans to issue second drafts or final versions. **Last checked:** 10 November 2026.

### Programme strategy and execution

**Audience:** Programme managers coordinating diagnosis, planning, and delivery.

**[Dutch PQC Migration Handbook](https://english.aivd.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2024/12/3/the-pqc-migration-handbook/The+PQC+Migration+Handbook+.pdf)** (AIVD, CWI, and TNO, December 2024). **Covers:** Diagnose, plan, and execute, with urgent adopters, regular adopters, and cryptography suppliers distinguished. **Assumes:** A national-strategy reader in the Netherlands or an organisation mirroring that model. **Leaves out:** Rewriting this handbook; this site links to it. **Status:** Current handbook. **Last checked:** 10 November 2026.

**[PQCC migration roadmap](https://pqcc.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/PQC-Migration-Roadmap-PQCC-2.pdf)**, May 2025. **Covers:** Four categories—preparation, baseline understanding, planning and execution, and monitoring and evaluation. **Assumes:** A multi-sector programme consortium reader. **Leaves out:** National legal obligations. **Status:** Current roadmap. **Last checked:** 10 November 2026.

**[ETSI TR 103 619](https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_tr/103600_103699/103619/01.01.01_60/tr_103619v010101p.pdf)**. **Covers:** Three stages—inventory, plan, execute—for public-key protections; quantum key distribution and quantum random-number generators are set aside. **Assumes:** European telecom and ICT standards context. **Leaves out:** Symmetric-only systems. **Status:** Current technical report. **Last checked:** 10 November 2026.

**[ETSI TR 104 016](https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_tr/104000_104099/104016/01.01.01_60/tr_104016v010101p.pdf)**, October 2024. **Covers:** Eleven annual steps for a programme office running a transition. **Assumes:** A standing programme office. **Leaves out:** First-day inventory tactics. **Status:** Current technical report. **Last checked:** 10 November 2026.

### Supplier and procurement questions

**Audience:** Procurement, legal or risk, and asset owners preparing supplier engagement.

**[Post-quantum questions to ask your vendors](https://www.cyber.gov.au/publication/post-quantum-questions-to-ask-your-vendors)** (ASD and ACSC, with CISA and NSA), updated 5 November 2026. **Covers:** Structured supplier questions, including embedded and hardware-bound cryptography among common concerns, and ASD's planning milestone at the end of 2030 for its audience. **Assumes:** Contract renewals and new purchases. **Leaves out:** Protocol-level test vectors. **Status:** Current guide. **Last checked:** 5 November 2026. **Handbook practice:** End of 2030 is ASD's planning milestone for its audience, not a universal deadline.

### National resource lists

**Audience:** Readers who need pointers beyond a single handbook.

**[Dutch government useful resources](https://www.nldigitalgovernment.nl/overview/quantum-safe-cryptography/useful-resources/)**. **Covers:** Curated Dutch-language and English pointers, including the handbook and the PQCC roadmap. **Assumes:** Readers already chose the Dutch handbook for strategy. **Leaves out:** Non-Dutch legal timelines. **Status:** Current web list. **Last checked:** 10 November 2026.
