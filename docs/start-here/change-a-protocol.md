## A weak answer

"We enabled PQC."

## A stronger answer

"We changed key establishment on one channel, the EU roadmap is the authority we are following for that high-risk system, and the rollback steps were written before the pilot."

The [EU NIS Cooperation Group roadmap](https://securitydelta.nl/media/com_hsd/report/750/document/Roadmap-on-postquantum-cryptography-PzBJxNUYyeuEdVUacWL696DofZQ-117507.pdf), 11 June 2025, is the authority named in that answer for a high-risk system. [Vista Security](https://vistasecurity.io/pqc-roadmap-small-midsize-businesses/), a vendor blog of 13 October 2026, is a sizing of writing the rollback before a pilot. It is not an authority, and this page does not copy a plan from that blog.
