The [CISA, NSA, and NIST fact sheet](https://www.nccoe.nist.gov/sites/default/files/2023-08/quantum-readiness-fact-sheet.pdf), as of 17 August 2023, starts from an inventory of quantum-vulnerable cryptography and from what you ask vendors. Handbook guidance: an assertion is the starting claim, and a configuration is what shows the change.

Supplier evidence packages are onward reading in the [Supply Chain Security Handbook](https://supplychainsecurityhandbook.com/practices-controls/supplier-assurance-procurement/). That link is not the authority for a post-quantum date.

## Outcome

The list of records: the inventory, the priority, the vendor answer, and what shipped.

## A weak answer

A slide that says quantum safe.

## A stronger answer

The inventory, the priority note, the tracking sheet, and a configuration or certificate that shows the change.
