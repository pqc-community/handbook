On 12 October 2026 Germany's Federal Ministry of the Interior (BMI), Federal Ministry for Digital Transformation and Government Modernisation (BMDS) and Federal Office for Information Security (BSI) published a joint guideline implementing the [EU PQC roadmap](/standards/migration-guides/eu-nis-roadmap/) nationally. It recommends that German administrations, organisations and companies, especially critical infrastructure operators, follow the roadmap's milestones for 2026, 2030 and 2035.

{/* truncate */}

## What the guideline says

The guideline, published in German as a [PDF](https://www.bsi.bund.de/SharedDocs/Downloads/DE/BSI/Krypto/Leitlinie-Umstellung-PQC.pdf?__blob=publicationFile) dated 11 September 2026, is addressed to federal, state and local administrations and to organisations and companies. It adopts the roadmap's timelines: the initial preparation and planning phase complete by the end of 2026, high-risk use cases quantum-safe by the end of 2030, and medium-risk use cases by the end of 2035.

Its deadlines are urgent recommendations rather than obligations. It asks bodies such as the IT Planning Council (IT-Planungsrat) and UP KRITIS, the public–private partnership for critical infrastructure, to adopt them as bindingly as possible by the end of 2026, and notes that the BSI has already set binding deadlines for approved products for classified information. It does not change the BSI's algorithm recommendations, which stay in [BSI TR-02102-1](/standards/national-algorithm-positions/bsi-tr-02102-1/).

## What it means for your plan

For organisations in Germany, the EU roadmap's dates now come with a national authority's recommendation behind them, and could become binding through the bodies the guideline names. Record them on [Set dates and milestones](/plan/programme/dates/) as the guideline's dates for its audience, alongside any sector or contract dates, and watch for IT-Planungsrat or UP KRITIS decisions that make them binding. The end-2026 milestone for initial preparation and planning is close: a programme that has [set up](/plan/programme/set-up/) its charter and started its inventory is working towards it.

Organisations outside Germany are not addressed, but the guideline shows how a Member State turns the EU roadmap into national guidance, and other Member States' national roadmaps are due under the same end-2026 milestone.
